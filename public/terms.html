Bookings

A request sent through this website is not a confirmed booking. Your booking is confirmed only when we confirm it by phone or WhatsApp from +91 87555 42743, or by email from adventurepark661@gmail.com. Rafting prices are listed per person on our website. Weekend prices, on Saturday and Sunday, are 20% higher. Camping and guest house prices depend on the season and weekend demand. Children aged 6 to 11 pay 50% of the adult camping price. We confirm the final price for your group when we confirm your booking.

Season and timings

Rafting and camping run from September to June. Rafting trips run between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Rafting and camping are closed in July and August during the monsoon. Our guest house is open all year. Our office is open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Safety rules

  • All participants must wear the life jacket and helmet provided, at all times on the river.
  • All participants must follow the instructions of the river guide.
  • Alcohol or drugs before or during any activity are not allowed. Anyone under the influence will not be allowed to take part.
  • Age, weight and health limits apply. Tell us about any medical condition, pregnancy or injury before your trip.
  • Participants may be asked to sign a risk acknowledgement form at registration.

Weather and river conditions

Adventure sports depend on nature. If the river level, weather or a government order makes an activity unsafe, we may change, delay or cancel it. We will offer you another slot or date.

Your belongings

Please do not bring jewellery or valuables on the river. We are not responsible for items lost or damaged during activities.
