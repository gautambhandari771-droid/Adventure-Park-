Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Adventure Park ("we", "us"), Badrinath Highway, near Shiv Mandir, Shivpuri, Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you send a booking request we collect your name, mobile number, optional email address, chosen activity, date, group size and any message you write. We also keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address to stop spam and abuse. We cannot turn it back into your real IP address.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or third-party scripts on this website.

Why we use it

  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your booking.
  • To plan your trip, including guides, rafts and camping.
  • To meet legal and safety record-keeping duties.

We never sell or rent your data, and we do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

How we protect it

The website uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Booking data is stored on a secured server and only our staff can see it, using a password-protected admin panel.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed for your trip and for our legal and accounting duties, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Email adventurepark661@gmail.com or call +91 87555 42743. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Cookies

Visitors get no cookies. A single security cookie is used only when our staff log in to the admin panel.
