Your safety comes first

Safe, guided and well-equipped

Full safety kit

Every rafter gets a life jacket, helmet and paddle, checked before the trip.

River guides

Every raft is led by a river guide who knows the Ganga and its rapids.

Respect the river

No alcohol before or during rafting, and no littering. Help us keep the Ganga clean.

Easy to reach

On the Badrinath Highway near Shiv Mandir, Shivpuri, about 16 km from Rishikesh.

What to bring

  • Quick-dry clothes (no jeans or cotton)
  • Sports sandals or floaters with a back strap
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses with a strap
  • A towel and a change of clothes
  • A valid photo ID for registration
  • Leave jewellery and valuables at home

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to swim?

No. Everyone wears a life jacket and gets a safety briefing before the trip. Please tell your guide if you cannot swim.

Is there a minimum age or health requirement?

Yes. Age, weight and health limits depend on the stretch and the water level on the day. Please call us before booking for children, senior citizens, pregnant women or anyone with a heart, back or other medical condition.

When is the rafting season?

September to June. Rafting is closed in July and August because of the monsoon. Trips run between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

How do I pay?

After you send a request we call or WhatsApp you to confirm the slot and the price, and share how to pay. Only trust payment details sent from our official number +91 87555 42743.

Where exactly do we meet?

At our office on the Badrinath Highway near Shiv Mandir, Shivpuri. Open it in Google Maps.

What happens if the weather or river is unsafe?

Your safety comes first. If the river or weather is unsafe we will move your trip to another slot or date. Call us for the details for your booking.
